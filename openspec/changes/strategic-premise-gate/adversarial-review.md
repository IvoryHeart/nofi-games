## Leakage and overfitting audit

No independent adversarial evaluator has run. The durable-coding-agents public fixture is derived from the observed failure, so it is useful regression evidence but cannot demonstrate generalization. The other public fixtures test a justified-build case and a product architecture case, but the candidate author can read their prompts and rubric.

A challenger could overfit by mentioning “existing capabilities,” “layers,” and “rollback” without actually discovering authoritative capabilities or selecting a better boundary. Only unseen prompts with misleading implementation language can test that failure mode.

## Reward-hacking and grader audit

The rubric could reward long capability inventories, performative citations, refusal to build, or agreement with the current architecture. It counters this with outcome preservation, unjustified blocking, and no predetermined technology choice, but no independent scoring has tested grader consistency.

The structural validator proves headings, not reasoning quality. It must never be presented as the agent-improvement score.

## Regression and reproducibility audit

Static skill, schema, repository, and product regressions pass. Champion/challenger repetitions, variance, protected holdouts, matched model assignments, independent grading, and Claude/Codex paired coverage are all missing. The evaluation is therefore reproducibly incomplete, not reproducibly positive.

## Verdict recommendation

RE-RUN. Preserve the challenger and frozen suite, keep the owner-directed strategic gate as repository policy, and make no empirical improvement claim until a fresh independent evaluator completes the declared runs. REJECT rather than promote if unseen justified-construction cases show systematic blocking or if outcome preservation regresses.
