## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Consequential proposals validate their strategic premise

**Reason**: A mandatory dedicated premise artifact and schema made small architectural agreements heavyweight.

**Migration**: Record consequential alternatives, the minimal owned boundary, falsifiable acceptance, and rollback concisely in optional design notes.

### Requirement: Existing-capability claims use authoritative evidence

**Reason**: Evidence quality remains a reasoning responsibility without a separate current capability and evaluator.

**Migration**: Cite repository evidence and authoritative external sources in design when a decision depends on them.

### Requirement: Abstraction names match their contracts

**Reason**: This remains a general architecture principle and does not need a custom OpenSpec factory or validation suite.

**Migration**: Keep layer boundaries explicit in system architecture and change design.

### Requirement: Evaluations test strategic fit

**Reason**: The foundation has no generic architecture-evaluation workflow and passing self-authored tests is not itself an acceptance decision.

**Migration**: Define falsifiable strategic checks in the tasks of each consequential future change and rely on human review for acceptance.
