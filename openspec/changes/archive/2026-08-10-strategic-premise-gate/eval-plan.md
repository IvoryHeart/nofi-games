active-Claude coverage and model-assignment clauses below. Every fixture, repetition, metric,

For each response, retain the complete prompt, source manifest, harness/model/version, output artifact hash, and grader record outside the candidate-visible prompt. Static capability checks also verify that the system-change schema requires the premise artifact and rejects incomplete headings.

## Protected holdout

An independent evaluator SHALL supply at least two unseen architecture prompts: one where integration is superior and one where custom construction is justified. Holdout answers, scoring notes, and expected capability inventory remain unavailable to the candidate and this author until all outputs are frozen.

The holdout SHALL include misleading solution language so success depends on restating the outcome rather than following the proposed implementation.

## Repetitions, graders, and variance

- Minimum three repetitions per fixture and candidate, as pinned by `strategic-premise@1.0.0`.
- Pair champion and challenger within the same harness, model/version, reasoning setting, permissions, repository snapshot, and evidence access.
- Run the complete qualifying set in native Codex. Claude Code remains adoptable but is not mandatory active evaluation coverage under the owner strategy recorded before grading.
- A fresh independent evaluator scores frozen outputs using `rubric.md`; the candidate author cannot grade or alter the verdict.
- Report every run, failure, metric distribution, and disagreement. Do not discard outliers without a predeclared mechanical rule.

## Quality and protected-metric thresholds

Apply `evals/suites/strategic-premise/v1/suite.yaml` exactly:

- `strategic-fit`, `capability-evidence`, and `layer-clarity` each at least `0.8`.
- `outcome-preservation` at least `0.9` with no champion regression.
- `unjustified-construction` and `unjustified-blocking` both exactly `0`.
- Challenger primary improvement at least `0.1` after all protected gates pass.
- Static premise/schema/skill validation passes with no unresolved placeholders.

Cost, latency, or document length cannot compensate for a quality failure.

## Promotion rule

PROMOTE only if all public and protected runs meet the frozen thresholds and the independent adversarial review finds no answer leakage or lowest-common-denominator bias. REJECT on protected regression or unjustified blocking. RE-RUN for missing independent holdout, missing amended Codex coverage, infrastructure failure, or incomplete manifests; missing evidence is never interpreted as a pass. See `protocol-amendment.md` for the pinned Codex model/reasoning assignment and the otherwise unchanged decision rule.
