# Product strategy

## Player promise

Nofi is one living game app: a curated catalog of original games that improves in response to trustworthy gameplay evidence.

## Studio thesis

Game production is cheap; reliable learning is scarce. Optimize for the number of well-tested product hypotheses converted into trustworthy player learning, not for code volume or raw games published.

## Catalog policy

The platform has no permanent genre roadmap. A research workflow gathers current demand, competitive density, audience needs, platform fit, production risks, and evaluation feasibility. It proposes multiple concepts and records why a concept should be built, tested, promoted, changed, or retired.

Fixture games exist only to validate contracts. They are never presented as market choices.

## Current scope

- One player app for web, desktop, Android, and iOS.
- Downloadable, versioned Godot game packs.
- Research, design, building, evaluation, publishing, observation, experimentation, and agent improvement.
- Gameplay quality, accessibility, safety, privacy, and reliable operation.

## Explicitly deferred

- Ads, purchases, currencies, marketplaces, loot boxes, or other monetization.
- Separate store applications for individual games.
- Console distribution.

## Success

The system succeeds when it repeatedly demonstrates this closed loop:

1. Research discovers an evidence-backed opportunity.
2. Multiple original concepts compete against explicit criteria.
3. A selected concept becomes a conforming game pack.
4. Independent evaluations find and help repair weaknesses.
5. The single app publishes the qualified pack to a controlled cohort.
6. Gameplay evidence yields a falsifiable improvement hypothesis.
7. A controlled game or agent change outperforms its pinned baseline.
8. Promotion or rollback is automatic, inspectable, and reversible.
