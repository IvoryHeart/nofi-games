# Product strategy

## Player promise

Nofi is one living game app: a curated catalog of original games that improves in response to trustworthy player evidence.

## Studio north star

The destination is an agent-operated studio that continuously researches opportunities, compares original concepts, creates conforming game packs, evaluates them, releases qualified versions through the single app, observes consented outcomes, and improves games and studio capabilities.

The first autonomous game run falsified the platform-v2 execution architecture, not this direction. Platform v3 begins with a green product substrate and explicit boundaries; it implements no autonomous studio loop yet.

## Studio thesis

Game production is cheap; reliable learning is scarce. Optimize for well-tested product hypotheses converted into trustworthy player learning, not code volume, agent activity, or raw games published.

## Catalog policy

The platform has no permanent genre roadmap. Any future research capability must use current demand, competitive density, audience needs, platform fit, production risks, and evaluation feasibility. Multiple materially different concepts compete before selection.

Fixtures validate contracts only. They are never market choices and remain non-discoverable.

## Autonomy and authority

Humans initially approve every authority-bearing action and permanently approve authority, delegation, evaluation, promotion, safety, and release policy itself. Changes to those policies are never delegable.

Humans may later delegate bounded acceptance, release, and promotion actions under previously accepted deterministic rules. Delegated execution must fail closed: missing, conflicting, stale, invalid, or ambiguous eligibility evidence performs no action and pauses for human review. An affected agent cannot approve or promote its own change.

This boundary enables routine autonomous operation without allowing agents to create authority, reinterpret policy, or lower their own promotion bar.

## Current foundation

- One Godot player app for web, desktop, Android, and iOS.
- Downloadable, versioned game-pack contract, canonical SDK, fixture, and build tooling.
- Product-only catalog and consented gameplay database schema.
- Lightweight OpenSpec agreements and deterministic repository checks.
- No autonomous workflow, registry, agent runtime, promotion engine, or run-record store.

## Explicitly deferred

- The autonomous studio workflow and its deterministic orchestration.
- Ads, purchases, currencies, marketplaces, loot boxes, or other monetization.
- Separate store applications for individual games.
- Console distribution.

## Success

The future system succeeds when it repeatedly and safely demonstrates:

1. Research discovers an evidence-backed opportunity.
2. Multiple original concepts compete against explicit criteria.
3. A selected concept becomes a conforming game pack.
4. Independent evaluation finds and helps repair weaknesses.
5. The single app releases an eligible pack to an authorized cohort.
6. Gameplay evidence yields a falsifiable improvement hypothesis.
7. A controlled change satisfies its previously accepted eligibility rules.
8. Deterministic orchestration promotes or rolls back within delegated authority, or fails closed to human review.

The v3 bootstrap succeeds earlier: the preserved player/pack substrate stays green, retired execution machinery is absent, and the future boundary is understandable without implying that the loop already exists.
